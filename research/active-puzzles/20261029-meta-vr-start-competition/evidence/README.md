## Verified prize schedule

Entertainment and Gaming: each has a new and an adapted division, each awarding $100,000 winner and $50,000 runner-up (total $600,000 across both tracks). Productivity: new and adapted divisions each award $70,000 and $35,000 (total $210,000). Six special awards at $25,000 each (including Best Agentic Interaction); two Judges' Choice at $20,000 each. Total maximum $1,000,000. Only one prize per entry; tax and payout requirements apply. No prize is guaranteed.

## Participant gates (NOT verified)

1. Confirm individual jurisdiction and legal eligibility; Meta Developer Access enabled, VR Start membership accepted, and Devpost join.
2. Confirm IWSDK/WebXR or native toolchain, hand-tracking compatibility, Quest 3/3S emulator/simulator, hosting, and public video workflow. No headset is required by rules, but functional compatibility is.
3. Confirm original project or materially updated project, no pre-existing Meta funding, third-party asset licenses, and English submission.
4. Confirm project accessible to judges through announcement; avoid any testing against third-party systems without explicit authorization.
